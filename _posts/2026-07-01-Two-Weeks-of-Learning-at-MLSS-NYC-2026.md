The second half of June was an intense and inspiring period for [Onur](https://www.rafalkucharskilab.pl/research/onur_akman/). He attended [**MLSS NYC 2026**](https://cfe.columbia.edu/content/mlss2) at Columbia University in New York. During the two-week summer school, he took part in lectures delivered by leading experts in machine learning, explored topics beyond his own research area, and exchanged ideas with early-career researchers from around the world. The experience brought new perspectives, valuable scientific connections, and fresh inspiration that will contribute to our ongoing ERC research. 🌍🧠
