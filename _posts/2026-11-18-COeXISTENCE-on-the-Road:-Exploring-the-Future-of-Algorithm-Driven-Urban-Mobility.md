What happens when autonomous vehicles become a significant part of urban traffic – and algorithms take the wheel?
📍Today, Rafał presented the latest results and findings of the COeXISTENCE project at the [**TRANSP-OR Seminar**](https://memento.epfl.ch/event/collective-and-strategic-routing-of-av-fleets-in-u/) at [EPFL in Lausanne](https://www.linkedin.com/school/epfl/home/). 📍The research journey continues next Monday, when he will present at the T&P Seminar at [Delft University of Technology](https://www.linkedin.com/school/tudelft/home/).

His presentations explore what might happen when autonomous vehicles reach a critical mass on our roads and their routes through already congested cities are increasingly determined by algorithms – sometimes with unexpected consequences.
From simplified macroscopic models, through the detailed microscopic simulator RouteRL, to large-scale experimental evaluation using the URB benchmark leaderboard, Rafał is sharing a range of novel findings and insights.
Some are intriguing, some potentially alarming, and others genuinely promising. Together, they offer a glimpse into the challenges and opportunities that algorithm-driven autonomous mobility may bring to our cities.
