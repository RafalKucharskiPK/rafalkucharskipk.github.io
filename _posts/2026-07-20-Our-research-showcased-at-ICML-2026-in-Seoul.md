We are excited to share that our team presented four research posters at [**the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2026 in Seoul**](https://icml.cc/) — one of the world’s leading conferences in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Our contributions focused on advancing multi-agent reinforcement learning, cooperative AI, and intelligent transportation systems, exploring how autonomous agents can learn, collaborate, and make better decisions in complex real-world environments.

Represented by [Anastasia Psarou](https://www.rafalkucharskilab.pl/research/anastasia_psarou/) and [Onur Akman](https://www.rafalkucharskilab.pl/research/onur_akman/), our team had the opportunity to discuss our latest results with researchers from around the world, exchange ideas, and receive valuable feedback from the machine learning community.
