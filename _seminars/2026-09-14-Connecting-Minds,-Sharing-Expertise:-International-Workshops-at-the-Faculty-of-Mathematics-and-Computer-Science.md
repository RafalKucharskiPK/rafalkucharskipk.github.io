We kicked off September on a high note, co-organising the _Research Meets Practice_ workshop&nbsp;with [**Centralny Port Komunikacyjny (CPK)**](https://portpolska.pl/pl/) at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science.
The event brought together researchers and transport professionals for three roundtables focused on transport modelling and optimisation, economics and tariffs, and travel behaviour and passenger data.
The discussions highlighted the role of AI, Big Data, robust models and practical expertise in addressing real-world transport challenges and shaping better transport decisions 🚆
A summary of the discussions and key insights can be found 👉[here](https://www.rafalkucharskilab.pl/assets/pdf/Podsumowanie%20warsztat%C3%B3w%20CPK2026.pdf)
