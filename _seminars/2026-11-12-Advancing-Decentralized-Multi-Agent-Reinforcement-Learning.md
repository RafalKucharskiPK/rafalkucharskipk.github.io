How can AI agents learn to cooperate effectively without relying on communication during execution?&nbsp;[Maciej Wojtala](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maciej-wojtala-3092392a9/) addressed this question in a seminar presenting [_Communication-Enhanced Tutoring for Efficient Decentralized Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13661), co-authored with Bogusz Stefańczyk, Dominik Bogucki, Łukasz Lepak, and Paweł Wawrzyński.
The presentation introduced a novel tutoring framework that enables agents to share information while learning, improving their performance and efficiency, while ultimately allowing them to act independently. The proposed approach achieved promising results on challenging benchmarks, including Hallway, SMAC, and SMACv2.
The work opens up new possibilities for building more efficient and truly decentralized multi-agent systems.
